# Research review

Identify the report claims that matter to the user's decision. Check whether the linked evidence supports their exact scope. Examine numerical claims with their units, baselines, and measurement conditions.

Check that the answer covers the material parts of the request, accounts for credible contrary results, and calibrates certainty to the evidence. A limitation explained at the point it matters can be an adequate answer.

For an actionable objection, identify the claim or omission, the observed evidence, the consequence, and a feasible correction. Distinguish essential corrections from optional improvements.
