# Research evidence

Start from the question and available leads. Find supporting and contrary evidence through terminology, citations, authors, official artifacts, and relevant source sections. In a supplied corpus, follow new leads within the available files and record any coverage gaps.

For each important claim, save the primary link and the section, table, or example that supports it. For numbers, record the unit, baseline, setup, and scope. Separate published measurements from estimates and your own arithmetic.

Check whether comparisons use equivalent conditions. Describe meaningful disagreements and state what additional evidence could resolve them. Return findings compactly enough for the scientist to compare across branches.
